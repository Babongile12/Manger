Manger: your group’s songs, kept.

Short description
Manger is a simple home for your choir or worship group’s songs, events and setlists, all in one place and private to your group.

Full description
Manger helps choirs, worship teams and music groups stay organised and ready to perform. Each group gets its own private space with a group username and password that members share. Songs and events are never visible to any other group.

Store every song with its lyrics, musical key and practice notes, such as “Bass 2 comes in on beat 3.” You can also record a short 5-second clip of the starting key, so singers can tap play and find their pitch right away. A search bar finds songs by title, key or lyrics.

Plan your performances by creating events with a date, time, venue, dress code and notes. Pick songs from your library and arrange them into a setlist with simple up and down arrows. The Home page always shows your next performance, with a countdown, the details and the setlist in order, so anyone can open the app and see what’s coming and what to sing. Tapping a song opens its lyrics, notes and recorded key.

Make it your own in Settings. You can rename your group, choose from five colour themes (Brown & Gold, Ocean Blue, Forest Green, Royal Purple and Night), change your password, log out, or reset the group to start afresh. Once you log in, Manger remembers you on that device.

Who it’s for
Church choirs, worship teams, youth groups, school choirs, gospel groups and any group that rehearses and performs together.

Key features

Private space for each group, protected by a group username and password
Song library with lyrics, key, practice notes and search
5-second recorded key for pitch reference
Events with date, time, venue, dress code and notes
Drag-free setlist ordering with arrowsa
Home page showing the next performance and its setlist
Five colour themes
Works on phones, tablets and computers
