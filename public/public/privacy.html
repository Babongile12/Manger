Manger – Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

Manger helps choirs, worship teams and music groups keep their songs, events, practices and members organised. This policy explains what information the app stores and why.

Who is responsible

[YOUR NAME / ORGANISATION], contact: [YOUR EMAIL]. Each group's leader decides what is added about their own group.

What we store

  • Group account: group name, group username and password (the password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see it in plain text).
  • Group content: songs (titles, lyrics, keys, notes, links, short audio clips), events, practices, setlists, announcements and polls.
  • Member details added by the group's leader: name, the part they sing, and optionally phone number, email address, birthday (day and month are used) and notes. Practice attendance and RSVP replies.
  • On your device: which member you chose as "you", your favourite songs, and notification settings.

Why we store it

Only to run the app for your group: showing songs and setlists, planning practices, recording attendance, and sending reminders and notifications to the members of that group.

Who can see it

Only people who have the group's username and password. Groups cannot see each other's data. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Where it is kept

On Google Firebase (Firestore) servers, which may be outside South Africa. Data is also cached on your device so the app works offline.

Your rights

You may ask your group's leader to view, correct or remove your details. Leaders can delete members and data inside the app. To have a whole group deleted, contact [YOUR EMAIL].

Children

Groups that include under-18s (e.g. youth choirs) should get a parent or guardian's consent before adding a child's personal details.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
